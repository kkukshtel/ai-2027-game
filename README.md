This is a dump from an experiment to get Gemini 2.5 to generate a strategy game based primarily off of [AI 2027](https://ai-2027.com/) and inspired by the rulesets behind Twilight Struggle, Imperial Struggle, and Daybreak. It was also provided with a few other papers about the geopolitics of developing AGI:  
[AI Behind Closed Doors: a Primer on The Governance of Internal Deployment](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2504.12170)  
[In Which Areas of Technical AI Safety Could Geopolitical Rivals Cooperate?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12914)

I wrote a blog post covering working on this here: BLOG

Things here likely conflict and aren't balanced or necessarilly internally consistent.
